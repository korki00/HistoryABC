HistoryABC Tools

A small private tool used only by the creator of the YouTube channel HistoryABC (@History-ABC) to manage that one channel.

What it does

  • Uploads the creator's own finished history Shorts to the HistoryABC channel and schedules them (YouTube Data API: videos.insert).
  • Adds the channel's videos to the channel's existing playlists (playlistItems.insert).
  • Reads view counts and YouTube Analytics reports of the channel's own videos for a private daily report.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is a local script on the owner's computer, not a public service. It does not access any other user's channel or data.
